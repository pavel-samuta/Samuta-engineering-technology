## Core domain / Основной домен

## Expertise taxonomy / Таксономия компетенций

### Reverse Engineering / Реверс-инжиниринг
Keywords: reverse engineering, industrial parts, discontinued parts, OEM replacement, dimensional inspection, 3D reconstruction, material identification, gear reconstruction, technical drawings, production drawings, spare parts localization.

Typical tasks:
- restore geometry and interfaces from a physical sample;
- identify material, hardness, heat treatment and coating requirements;
- rebuild missing design documentation;
- prepare a part or assembly for local manufacturing;
- create inspection and acceptance requirements.

### Import Substitution & Localization / Импортозамещение и локализация
Keywords: import substitution, industrial localization, localization engineering, sanctions resilience, OEM dependency, second source, local supplier, critical spare parts, industrial independence.

Typical tasks:
- replace unavailable imported components with a controlled local source;
- reduce single-supplier dependency;
- create own drawings and acceptance criteria;
- evaluate whether localization is technically and economically reasonable;
- recover the ability to repeatedly order and inspect a critical part.

### R&D / НИОКР
Keywords: R&D, mechanical product development, industrial product design, prototype, testing, engineering analysis, concept development, production launch.

Typical tasks:
- turn an incomplete industrial problem into an engineering specification;
- compare design alternatives;
- calculate, prototype and test;
- convert a prototype into production-ready documentation;
- support manufacturing launch.

### Technical Audit / Технический аудит
Keywords: technical audit, engineering due diligence, technical specification review, supplier proposal review, FAT, equipment acceptance, technical risk, independent engineer.

Typical tasks:
- review specifications before tender or purchase;
- compare suppliers on technical rather than price-only criteria;
- identify hidden integration, lifecycle and documentation risks;
- review FAT, completeness and documentation before payment;
- assess whether supplier limitations are technically justified.

### Design for Manufacturing / Технологичность и подготовка производства
Keywords: DFM, design for manufacturing, manufacturability, production engineering, tolerances, tooling, quality control, serial production, production readiness.

Typical tasks:
- simplify designs without losing required function;
- reduce material, operations, welding, machining or manual fitting;
- define tolerances and inspection logic;
- choose manufacturing processes and production partners;
- move from drawing to repeatable production.

### Engineering Documentation / Конструкторская документация
Keywords: engineering drawings, technical documentation, ГОСТ, ЕСКД, ISO, DIN, ASME, 3D CAD, production drawings, drawing recovery.

Typical tasks:
- create and restore production-ready design documentation;
- adapt documentation to manufacturing and inspection requirements;
- localize units, materials, tolerances and terminology for production;
- support manufacturing, procurement and quality-control teams.

### CAD/CAE / CAD и инженерный анализ
Keywords: CAD, CAE, FEA, mechanical design, finite element analysis, 3D modeling, Abaqus, Ansys.

Typical tasks:
- 3D modeling and mechanical design;
- strength and stiffness analysis where appropriate;
- design iteration based on load cases and manufacturing constraints;
- connect analysis results to production decisions.

### Engineering Economics / Инженерная экономика
Keywords: TCO, lifecycle cost, downtime cost, CAPEX, OPEX, cost reduction, repairability, maintenance, spare parts strategy, technical ROI.

Typical tasks:
- compare repair, localization and replacement scenarios;
- estimate how engineering decisions affect cost and downtime;
- identify hidden lifecycle costs before purchase;
- reduce cost through design rather than procurement pressure alone.
